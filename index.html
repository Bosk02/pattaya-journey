Pattaya Thailand

Pattaya can be whatever you want it to be. Most people seem to have one view of the city and that view is limited. Pattaya can be an all night out of control party or Pattaya can be a quiet day at a cafe looking at the ocean with hardly a person around. Pattaya is what you want it to be.

Central Pattaya

Thailand is just wonderful. The people are friendly, kind, and generous. For nine months of the year it is one of the best places to live or visit. Skip January, February and March do to air pollution. Arrive in April and depart by the end of December.


Jomtien Pattaya

Jomtien is where I live. Miles long beach with condos, hotels and cafes up and down beach road. While it is much quieter than Central Pattay and the Walking Street area, parts of Jomtien can still be very busy, especially on the weekend.

Koh Larn

Koh Larn is just off the coast of Pattaya. You can easily see it from either Central Pattaya or from Jomtien. The Bali Hai Pier at the end of Walking Street is where you either catch a ferry or a speed boat to get to Koh Larn. A speed boat will take about 20 minues and a ferry about 40 minutes. Avoid the beach where the ferry drops you off. This is a horribly over crowded and unpleasant area. Take the Baht Bus and go to the other beaches and you will be happy.


The beauty of Thailand

Many people come to Thailand and leave unhappy. They spent more money than they expected. Their heart was broken by a Thai girl they were in love with. The extreme heat combined with excess drinking of alcohol caused them to have a medical problem. These are common events in Thailand.

Thailand can however, be an amazing place for you to spend your time. Its all about know what you are getting into and understanding the risks and traps.

The risks and the traps are obvious and clear. The problem is that so many people come here and tell themselve that they are not ever going to fall for them. Sadly this belief helps so many fall into dangerous traps.

Come to Thailand with a good attitude. Come to Thailand and eat right and exercise. If you drink alcohol, then drink very little. If you want a Thai girlfriend or wife, then understand that it is a business transaction and not love. Follow these basic rules and you are well on your way to enjoying one of the best, most stress free lifestyles you can possibly hope for.

Travel Philosphy – While in Thailand I happend to have conversation with a lady at a beach cafe on two different days. These little chats were eye opening to me. We traded mostly travel stories. Eventually I noticed that her stories always involved many more places and activities than mine.

On the second day, I told her that the week I spent in Prague was amazing and that I wanted to return for a much longer stay and explore the city and country further. I told her that I was considering a month or longer. This seemed to really confused her. She asked me “Why would you want to go back to a place you have already travelled to?”.

Now it was my turn to be confused. I thought the answer was obvious. I told her “Because there was so much more to see and do and I want to spend time in a place that I enjoy so very much.” It was her opinion that I should go to new countries and not ones I had already visited.

After our chat was finished, I realized that this woman had every day of her trips filled with as many activities as possible and that she was jumping around the world from one place to the next at a very quick pace, never spending too long anywhere. It was almost as if she need to check things off a list as quickly as possible.

I prefer to find a few places I like and spend more time in those places.

The importance of travelling either alone or with the right person – She and I would never be successful traveling together. Her rush to go to as many places as possible and have as many experiences as possible would clash with my desire to go slow and enjoy things longer. If you are going to travel with someone, it seems to me that you need to be very careful in picking who that person is, otherwise you may end up trapped in a very unpleasant trip,
